1. ABOUT THE COMPANY

Southern Cross Retail Group is an Australian retail organization operating across lifestyle products, homewares, and personal accessories. The company serves customers through a combination of physical retail stores and online shopping channels.

Our teams work collaboratively to deliver consistent customer experiences, support product launches, and develop marketing initiatives that reflect customer needs and business objectives.

1.1 Our Values
1. Customer Focus: Delivering useful products and positive customer experiences.
2. Integrity: Communicating honestly and acting responsibly.
3. Collaboration: Working effectively across departments.
4. Accountability: Taking ownership of responsibilities and deadlines.
5. Continuous Improvement: Learning from customer feedback and business performance.

2. POSITION OVERVIEW: MARKETING COORDINATOR

The Marketing Coordinator will support the planning, execution, and evaluation of marketing campaigns across digital and traditional channels. The role involves coordinating campaign materials, maintaining schedules, preparing content, and assisting with marketing performance reports.

The successful candidate will work closely with the Marketing Manager, design team, sales department, and retail operations team.

2.1 Key Responsibilities
1. Assist with planning seasonal promotions and marketing campaigns.
2. Prepare content for email newsletters and social media.
3. Coordinate campaign materials with internal teams and external suppliers.
4. Maintain the marketing calendar and monitor deadlines.
5. Assist with product launches and promotional events.
6. Conduct basic market and competitor research.
7. Track campaign performance and prepare reports.
8. Ensure marketing materials follow established brand guidelines.
9. Provide administrative support to the marketing team.

2.2 Essential Requirements
* A degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills.
* Good organizational and time-management abilities.
* Attention to detail and the ability to manage competing priorities.
* Confidence working collaboratively with different teams.
* Basic familiarity with social media and digital marketing platforms.

Note: Previous marketing experience, knowledge of email marketing tools, and familiarity with campaign analytics are desirable but not mandatory.

3. RECRUITMENT PROCESS

The recruitment process consists of three stages. Progression depends on the outcome of each stage, and candidates will receive further instructions from the recruitment team.

3.1 Stage 1: Initial Recruitment Interview
* Duration: Approximately 20 minutes  
* Format: Video call or telephone interview  
* Purpose: Evaluates candidate's background, communication skills, interest in the role, and general suitability.

Example Questions:
1. Could you introduce yourself and describe your professional background?
2. Why are you interested in this Marketing Coordinator position?
3. What interests you about working in retail marketing?
4. How do you organize your work when managing several deadlines?
5. What skills would you like to develop in this role?

3.2 Stage 2: Marketing Interview
* Duration: Approximately 45 minutes  
* Format: Video call or in-person interview  
* Purpose: Evaluates practical marketing knowledge, problem-solving, communication, and teamwork.

Example Questions:
1. How would you plan a promotional campaign for a new product?
2. How would you identify an appropriate target audience?
3. Which metrics would you use to evaluate a social media campaign?
4. What would you do if a campaign was not meeting its targets?
5. How would you coordinate campaign materials with a designer and sales team?
6. How would you respond to negative customer feedback online?

Note: Candidates may also be asked to discuss relevant academic projects, previous work, or volunteer experience.

3.3 Stage 3: Final Interview
* Duration: Approximately 30 minutes  
* Format: Video call or in-person interview  
* Purpose: Focuses on teamwork, working preferences, availability, and expectations for the position.

Example Questions:
1. Describe a time you worked with others to complete a task.
2. How do you respond to constructive feedback?
3. How do you prioritize urgent and important tasks?
4. What type of working environment helps you perform well?
5. What questions do you have about the role or team?

4. INTERVIEW PREPARATION

Candidates are encouraged to review the position description and prepare examples that demonstrate their skills and experience.

Before the interview, candidates should:
1. Prepare a brief professional introduction.
2. Review relevant education, employment, and project experience.
3. Prepare examples of communication, organization, and teamwork.
4. Consider how they would approach basic marketing scenarios.
5. Prepare questions about the role and team.
6. Check the interview invitation, date, time, and meeting link.
7. Join the meeting a few minutes before the scheduled start.

Candidates should provide honest answers and clearly explain their own contributions to any projects they discuss.

1. The candidate submits the required information.
2. The system validates the submitted details.
3. The requested slot is checked against existing bookings.
4. If the slot is available, the booking is recorded.
